# Ready-to-upload submission for QPAIN 2027

`QPAIN2027_submission_blind.pdf` is the file to upload. It is the conference paper with no author block and no repository link (QPAIN review is double-blind), 6 pages, A4, IEEE conference format, fonts embedded. The LaTeX source is in `qpain2027_blind/` (compile with pdfLaTeX, BibTeX, pdfLaTeX, pdfLaTeX).

## What you still have to do yourself (about 15 minutes)
1. Open the submission system: https://myproconf.com/events/app/login.php?url=qpain-2027 and create an account (this cannot be done for you).
2. Start a new submission, upload `QPAIN2027_submission_blind.pdf`, paste the title and abstract from the PDF, and add the author details in the form (names are entered in the form, not in the PDF).
3. Choose Track 03 (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing and Understanding) if the form asks for a track.
4. Submit before **20 December 2026**. Keep the confirmation email.
5. No payment is needed now. If the paper is accepted (notification 5 Feb 2027), register and pay by 20 Feb 2027 (see `../SUBMISSION_OPTIONS.md`: local student author, non-IEEE, 95 USD, payable in taka by bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay, bank transfer or card).
6. For the camera-ready version, put the real author names back (uncomment the author block in `main.tex`) and add the repository link back.

## Before you submit, check on the official page
Page limit 4 to 6 pages (the paper has 6), IEEE conference template, double-blind, and whether a student author needs proof. See https://qpain.org/call-for-papers
